Website Review
& Improvements

$650 / one-time project

A focused review and practical updates to make your existing website clearer and easier to use.

  • Up to five existing pages reviewed
  • Up to ten prioritized recommendations
  • Two hours of agreed improvements and checking
  • A 45-minute walkthrough
Explore the website service

The review: Messaging, navigation, and how easily customers can find information and take the next step. You receive a concise list of priorities and the reasons behind them.

The improvements: One agreed batch of small changes, such as button wording, links, image swaps, or minor edits to existing text. The two-hour allowance includes implementation and checking; the number of updates depends on complexity.

Before we start: We confirm website access and platform suitability. You provide needed materials and approve the changes.

New pages, substantial copywriting, redesigns, and technical troubleshooting are quoted separately. This is not a comprehensive technical, SEO, or accessibility audit.

Need a larger refresh? Broader content, layout, or structure changes are quoted separately. We build on any review already completed so you don’t pay for the same assessment twice.

Social Media Support

$650 / month

Turn your photos, business updates, and promotions into a consistent social presence, from planning to publishing.

  • A monthly content calendar
  • Six posts with captions and simple graphics
  • One platform or coordinated Instagram + Facebook
  • Scheduling, a results summary, and a 30-minute call
Explore social media support

Your content calendar reflects your priorities, promotions, and seasonal needs. Posts use your existing photos and brand materials and include up to two simple carousels of three slides each.

Coordinated Instagram and Facebook means sharing the same six core posts across both platforms, rather than six unique posts per platform. Content is scheduled after your approval.

You provide photos, business updates, and timely feedback. One consolidated revision round is included.

Photography, video production, paid advertising, and daily comment or inbox management are not included. Additional work is quoted separately.

Multi-Channel Marketing Support

$800 / month

Bring your business updates, promotions, and upcoming events together with a monthly plan and hands-on help across two channels. Your selected channels determine the content and updates included.

  • A monthly plan across your two selected channels
  • Coordinated messaging and agreed hands-on deliverables
  • For example: four social posts + two emails
  • A results review and a 45-minute call
Explore scope & example combinations

We agree on your two channels and monthly priorities, then I prepare the included work and publish or schedule it after your approval. Social + email includes four social posts and two emails. Social + website includes four social posts and one website update batch. Email + website includes two emails and one website update batch. Your proposal confirms the combination; these are alternatives, not cumulative allowances.

Four social posts + two emails

Four social posts plus two emails, such as a newsletter and workshop announcement, or an invitation and reminder.

Email scope: Two emails per month when email is one of your selected channels. Each includes up to 400 words, a subject line, and a call to action, drafted from your supplied business updates. Includes formatting in an existing template, one audience per send, link checks, scheduling after approval, and one consolidated revision round. The emails can cover different topics, such as a newsletter and workshop announcement, or an invitation and reminder. Additional emails and other work are scoped and quoted separately, with approval before work begins. Original research, new templates, automations, and list cleanup are quoted separately.

Website scope: An agreed batch of text, image, or link updates on a supported platform, capped at 90 minutes. Redesigns, new features, and technical repairs are separate.

You supply existing assets, business information, access, and timely approval. One consolidated revision round is included. We review available results to inform the next month’s priorities.

Revisions & Approvals

One consolidated round of revisions is included each month. You'll have the opportunity to review all proposed content and provide feedback before anything is published.

Minor corrections to approved content, such as fixing typos or factual errors introduced by AlpineVale, are always included.

Additional revision rounds, significant changes in direction, or new requests outside the agreed scope may be quoted separately.

Need additional help? Additional social content, email production, Google Business Profile work, and website updates can be scoped around your needs.
