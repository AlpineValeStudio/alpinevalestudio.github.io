Common questions

About projects, pricing, and working together.

View questions

What can you help my business with?

I help with website improvements, social media, email marketing, and marketing planning. Whether you need help with one project or ongoing support, we’ll start with your priorities and agree on a clear scope.

I’m not sure what I need. Where should I start?

You don’t need a finished plan before reaching out. A free 20-minute introductory call gives us a chance to discuss your business, what’s working, and where you could use support. From there, I’ll recommend a practical next step.

Can you improve my existing website?

Yes. You may only need clearer messaging, simpler navigation, or a more obvious way for customers to contact you. I’ll review your website platform and access before we agree on improvements. Larger refreshes are scoped separately.

Do you work with businesses throughout Snohomish County?

Yes. AlpineVale Studio supports small businesses in Snohomish, Lake Stevens, and throughout Snohomish County. We can collaborate remotely, making it easier to fit marketing support into your schedule.

Can I hire you for a single project?

Yes. You can start with a website review, a focused marketing working session, or a separately scoped project. Monthly support is available when you need ongoing help.

What will you need from me?

Usually, a little background on your business, your goals, and any existing brand materials, photos, or content. Depending on the project, I may also need access to your website or marketing tools. We’ll agree on what’s needed before work begins.

Will I get to review content before it goes live?

Yes. Social posts and emails are scheduled after your approval. For website work, we’ll agree on the changes before implementation. Your proposal will explain the review process and included revisions.

Can you guarantee more customers or a higher Google ranking?

No one can promise a specific ranking or number of new customers. My focus is on making your marketing clearer, more consistent, and easier for people to act on, then using available results to guide the next steps.
