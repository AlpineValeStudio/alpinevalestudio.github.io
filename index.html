A few useful details

How do we get started?

Start with a complimentary 15–20 minute fit call. We’ll talk about what you need, then I’ll prepare a proposal with the agreed scope, timing and price. No service is booked until we’ve agreed on it.

What costs are separate?

All prices are in USD. Applicable taxes, ad spend, domains, hosting, paid tools and outside specialists are separate and agreed in advance. Photography and video production can be scoped separately.

What if the scope changes?

I’ll quote any additions before starting. Extra work is $125 per hour, with a one-hour minimum, or a separately agreed fixed fee. Revisions stay within the original brief.
